Applied Materials in India

India is not the back office. It never was.

Applied Materials India has been part of the global innovation network for more than two decades. Headquartered in Bengaluru, the organisation spans R&D, product engineering, design and validation.

Applied Materials describes India as a strategic innovation engine, part of the core global network, not a satellite or support centre.

The chips behind everything

Applied Materials works at the physical limits of what’s possible, engineering materials at the atomic scale to improve chip performance, power efficiency and yield. This is the most fundamental layer of semiconductor manufacturing.

From deposition and etch to CMP, Applied Materials’ process technologies are used in the manufacture of virtually every advanced semiconductor made today. If you’ve spent your career in process engineering, you already know this world.

Applied Materials carries one of the largest R&D investments in the semiconductor equipment industry. The tools that make next-generation chips possible are invented here, often years before the industry knows it needs them.

AI infrastructure, mobile devices, EVs, advanced communications, the chips that run the modern world are built with Applied Materials technology. The scale of that impact is what makes this a company worth understanding beyond the job description.
